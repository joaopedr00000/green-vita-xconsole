## English

### Front touch L3/R3 — update to the existing 0.3.3 release

The fourth per-game control option, **L3/R3 on front touch (lower half)**, is now available. Enable it in the individual game's settings. The lower 50% of the front screen is split into invisible left (L3) and right (R3) zones. The lower rear touch panel is free; the upper rear zones retain L2/R2 or L1/R1 according to the existing swap setting and rear-touch enable switch. The front upper half has no gameplay touch action in this mode. Front touch remains available in menus. The option defaults off and overrides front mirroring without erasing the previous preference. Both Gamepad RAW and SDL2 fallback support this layout; existing controls and remapping are preserved.

The tester confirmed that the new layout worked perfectly. The current VPK is the exact successful test artifact from [Actions #45784978910](https://github.com/joaopedr00000/green-vita-xconsole/actions/runs/45784978910), without recompilation. The previous 0.3.3 package and checksum are retained as `green-vita-original.vpk` and `green-vita-original.vpk.sha256`. No changes to the video/audio pipeline, latency policy or version number. Benchmarks below are historical results from the original 0.3.3, not newly measured for the touch update.

### A major step forward in low-latency cloud gaming on PS Vita

Version 0.3.3 is the stable culmination of the latency lab, developed and tested on actual PS Vita hardware. Rather than lowering resolution or intentionally discarding H.264 reference frames, this release removes unnecessary waiting between decoding and presentation, improves native controller dispatch, and refines audio buffering. The tester reports excellent image quality, no visible artifacts or frame problems in the tested sessions, noticeably more responsive controls, and stable sound in the final Flight Simulator test.

### Video and presentation
- **Extreme synchronization is universal:** the tested Extreme policy is now mandatory for every quality profile. The Classic/Adaptive/Extreme selector has been removed; old saved pacing choices are ignored. Low Latency, Balanced and Quality remain independent selectable streaming profiles.
- Frame-ready notifications let the renderer wake when a decoded picture is available. During cached, noninteractive playback, a nonblocking texture claim can present the picture directly before returning to normal application maintenance. Only one direct presentation is attempted per maintenance iteration; normal event, input and audio servicing remains.
- Adaptive output queue recovery prioritizes recent decoded pictures. Compressed H.264 access-unit ordering and reference handling are preserved; no deliberate reference-frame skipping, extra video texture, clock increase or new GPU synchronization assumption was introduced.
- Cached HUD geometry and late texture selection from 0.3.2 remain in place. Hardware H.264 decoding and the tested 1280×720 received stream are retained, scaled to the Vita's 960×544 display. The advertised 1920×1080 server output is distinct from the actual received resolution.
- Adaptive bitrate profiles remain available, with up to 15 Mbps requested in Quality. Requested bitrate is not guaranteed delivered throughput.

### Controller, network and stability
- **Gamepad RAW** remains automatic on supported handheld input. State changes notify the RTC worker directly; the periodic polling path remains as fallback.
- Brief native input lock contention no longer immediately forces SDL2 when a recent RAW dispatch is available. SDL2 remains the compatibility path for genuinely unavailable native input, external-controller scenarios and relevant system gestures. Digital edge acknowledgement and stale neutral-state retry handling are improved.
- Input telemetry distinguishes recent sample dispatch from observed state-change-to-accepted-data-channel timing. Neither measures physical button-to-screen latency.
- Network feedback adapts NACK grace conservatively between 12 and 18 ms after sufficient evidence of unrequested late arrivals; Low Latency and IDR recovery retain 12 ms. The RTP assembler's reordering windows and compressed video queue capacity are unchanged.
- Distinct counters for late arrivals, expired tracked gaps, untracked gaps, audio failures and missed telemetry help diagnose problems. A recovered gap is not evidence of permanent packet loss.
- Redundant SDL reads, intermediate pulse allocation and repeated expiry scans are reduced. Existing thread affinity and Extreme decoder-priority request remain; actual scheduling, battery and thermal gains are not established.

### Audio
- Stereo Opus at 48 kHz, with the existing requested SDL device period of 1024 samples, is preserved.
- Adaptive initial software-buffer targets: 40 ms (Low Latency), 40 ms (Balanced), 60 ms (Quality). During stable playback, targets may fall toward 40/40/50 ms respectively, subject to queue headroom. On insufficient refill, the target increases by 10 ms, capped at 110 ms.
- Drain already-decoded PCM before deciding to pause/rebuffer. If at least one negotiated device period is restored after an empty software queue, playback can continue without raising the target unnecessarily.
- Gentle stereo interpolation shortens excess queued audio by up to 1% at target +8 ms and 2% at target +20 ms. Disabled during startup/refill; no new abrupt queue-clearing behavior. Small speed/pitch changes are possible during correction.
- HUD distinguishes target from actual SDL software queue occupancy. Logs include accepted trim, refill counts and negotiated device period. These are not physical audio latency or a measured A/V offset.
- Final hardware test: the tester reports stable audio without interruptions, with previously almost imperceptible audio delay.

### Diagnostics and built-in benchmark
Six HUD pages: Summary, Advanced Network, Video and Audio, Input, Technical Overview, and **60s Benchmark**. Includes actual UDP throughput, ICE RTT, local pipeline stages, presentation interval P95, RAW input source, audio queue and recovery counters.
The automatic benchmark starts from the first presented picture, uses 10 seconds of warmup and 60 seconds of measurement, and reports minimum, mean, median, P95, P99, maximum, frame interval, decode-to-claim wait, successful CPU-side presentations and direct-presentation count. It resets after pause or a new session. Bounded storage reports any omitted samples. Results are also written to the bounded diagnostic log at `ux0:data/xcloud-rust/connection-status.txt`.

### Installation and validation
1. Download `green-vita.vpk` below and optionally verify `green-vita.vpk.sha256`.
2. Enable Unsafe Homebrew in HENkaku Settings on a homebrew-enabled PS Vita.
3. Install the VPK with VitaShell; it uses the existing GREENVITA title ID and replaces an installed version. Keep a backup of settings and the previous VPK.
4. Sign in with an Xbox account with access to Xbox Cloud Gaming, select your quality profile and play. No synchronization setting is required.
5. For diagnostics, enable the HUD in Settings, select 60s Benchmark, and play uninterrupted for at least 70 seconds.

### Um grande avanço na latência do streaming de jogos no PS Vita

A 0.3.3 Final reúne as otimizações do laboratório de latência, desenvolvidas e testadas em um PS Vita real. Em vez de reduzir a resolução ou descartar intencionalmente referências H.264, esta versão elimina esperas desnecessárias entre decodificação e apresentação, aprimora os controles e ajusta o áudio. O testador relatou excelente qualidade de imagem, ausência de artefatos ou problemas visíveis de quadros nas sessões testadas, resposta dos comandos perceptivelmente melhor e áudio estável no teste final com Flight Simulator.

### Vídeo e apresentação
- **Sincronização Extrema universal:** a política Extrema é obrigatória em todos os perfis de qualidade. O seletor Clássico/Adaptativo/Extremo foi retirado e preferências antigas de sincronização são ignoradas. Os perfis Baixa latência, Equilibrado e Qualidade continuam disponíveis.
- O decoder sinaliza a chegada de uma textura pronta. Durante a reprodução com HUD em cache e sem interação, o renderizador pode adquiri-la sem bloquear e apresentá-la diretamente, antes de voltar ao ciclo normal do aplicativo. Limite de uma apresentação direta por iteração de manutenção, preservando eventos, áudio e controles.
- Recuperação adaptativa da fila prioriza imagens decodificadas recentes. A ordem das unidades H.264 comprimidas e o tratamento de referências são preservados. Sem descarte intencional de referências, textura extra, overclock ou nova hipótese de sincronização da GPU.

### Controles, rede e estabilidade
- **Gamepad RAW** automático no controle nativo compatível. Mudanças de estado notificam diretamente a thread RTC; a verificação periódica continua disponível.
- Disputas momentâneas pelo acesso às amostras RAW não provocam imediatamente a troca para SDL2 quando houve despacho RAW recente. SDL2 permanece como fallback real e caminho de compatibilidade. Melhorias na confirmação das transições digitais e no reenvio do estado neutro quando a entrada fica obsoleta.
- Diagnóstico distingue o despacho da amostra recente do intervalo entre mudança observada e aceitação pelo canal de dados. Nenhum dos dois é a latência física botão-tela.
- Espera para NACK adaptativa entre 12 e 18 ms após evidência suficiente de chegadas atrasadas sem solicitação. Baixa latência e recuperação IDR conservam 12 ms. Janelas de reordenação RTP e capacidade da fila comprimida intactas.
- Contadores separados para atrasados, gaps expirados/não rastreados, falhas de áudio e amostras de telemetria perdidas. Um gap recuperado não comprova perda permanente.
- Menos leituras SDL redundantes, alocações intermediárias e verificações de expiração. Afinidade das threads e tentativa de prioridade elevada do decoder mantidas. Ganhos de bateria, temperatura e agendamento não foram medidos.

### Áudio
- Opus estéreo a 48 kHz e período SDL solicitado de 1024 amostras preservados.
- Alvos iniciais do buffer: 40 ms (Baixa latência), 40 ms (Equilibrado), 60 ms (Qualidade). Durante reprodução estável, podem cair até 40/40/50 ms, respectivamente, se houver margem na fila. Recuperação insuficiente aumenta o alvo em 10 ms, até 110 ms.

Las capturas de la 0.3.2 tenían una media de 50,05 ms; las primeras pruebas sin recuperación de cola alcanzaron unos 133,4 ms. Los 4,99 ms finales son aproximadamente un 90% y un 96% inferiores, respectivamente, como comparación orientativa, no una prueba A/B controlada.

**Importante:** la latencia local mide desde el encolado de la unidad comprimida hasta el retorno de la presentación del cliente. No incluye el ensamblaje RTP anterior, procesamiento del servidor, tránsito por la red ni actualización física de la pantalla. P95/P99 describen la ventana medida. RTT ICE y envío local de entrada son métricas separadas, no la latencia total de juego. Una comparación botón-pantalla requiere una cámara externa de alta velocidad.

### Instalación y validación
1. Descarga `green-vita.vpk` y verifica opcionalmente `green-vita.vpk.sha256`.
2. Activa Unsafe Homebrew en los ajustes HENkaku de una PS Vita preparada para homebrew.
3. Instala con VitaShell. El mismo Title ID GREENVITA sustituye la versión instalada; conserva el VPK anterior y una copia de los ajustes.
4. Inicia sesión con una cuenta Xbox con acceso a Cloud Gaming, elige el perfil de calidad y juega. No es necesario configurar la sincronización.
5. Para medir, activa el HUD, selecciona Benchmark 60 s y juega al menos 70 segundos sin pausar.

Pruebas automatizadas de controles, políticas, HUD y benchmark y compilación VitaSDK aprobadas.
